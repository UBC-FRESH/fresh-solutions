Goal:
Define the problem-adapter boundary and replay contract before extracting the solver.

Scope:
- Variables, domains, candidate/option representation, exact and batched evaluation.
- Feasibility, repair, move/undo, state initialization and application.
- Evidence and replay metadata.
- A toy adapter that does not import WS4.

Out of scope:
- Full engine extraction.
- GPU kernels.
- External benchmark adapters.

Architecture notes:
The engine must not assume forestry accounts, spatial adjacency, additive objectives, or that a batched evaluator exists.

- [ ] P1.3 Build a WS4-free toy adapter

- The adapter contract names exact and batched evaluation explicitly.
- The replay contract records instance, config, seed, and schedule.
